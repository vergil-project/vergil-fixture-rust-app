# Release 0.1.6 (2026-11-23)

## Chores

- **cargo update**

## Features

- **add the median subcommand, and roll memchr back in Cargo.lock (#77)**
  Prints the median of the integers it is given, the mean of the two middle ones rounded toward zero for an even count, and an error for none, with its tests and docs. Rolls the transitive memchr back from 2.8.3 to 2.8.2 in Cargo.lock only, so the release's dependency update has an upgrade to make.
